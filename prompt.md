Create a single self-contained HTML file (inline CSS, no JavaScript, Google Fonts allowed) for a visually striking, landscape web landing page that also works as a background graphic.

**Look and feel**
- Deep carbon background: a dark carbon-fibre weave pattern made with CSS gradients, a faint engineering grid that fades toward the edges, soft colour glows, and a dark vignette.
- Fonts: Space Grotesk for headings and body text, JetBrains Mono for small labels.
- Each decade gets its own accent colour: 1990s amber (#f5a524), 2000s ember (#ff6b4a), 2010s cyan (#3ec7ff), 2020s violet (#a77bff).

**Header**
- Small monospace eyebrow line above the title: "Four decades · IT & Architecture".
- Large title "Larryhere Operations Ltd". "Larryhere" stands out with a bold amber → ember → cyan → violet gradient and a soft glow. "Operations Ltd" is plain light grey (#c3cad4) in a light weight.
- Directly below the title, a monospace contact line with small diamond bullets: info@larryhere.com (as a mailto link) and Calgary, Alberta, Canada.
- A short muted tagline: "From control rooms and databases to enterprise architecture and AI — practical delivery across every layer of the stack."

**Timeline**
- A horizontal glowing rail with a gradient through the four decade colours, a glowing node above each decade, and four glass-effect cards side by side (frosted, with a top border in the decade colour).
- Each card shows a large glowing decade number, a small monospace label, and a list with rotated-square bullets in the decade colour:
  - **1990s — Foundations:** IT Management; Data Center Management; Software Development; Databases; SCADA, SNMP, KPI (one line); Business Applications; Desktop Support; Process Simulation
  - **2000s — Energy & Data:** Entrepreneurship; Contracting; Application Development; Oil & Gas — Upstream / Midstream / Downstream; Environmental; Solution Architecture; Data Governance; Data Integration & Migration; Process Modeling & Optimization
  - **2010s — Architecture:** Business Analysis; Enterprise Architecture; Technical Architecture; Web Development; Regulatory Reporting; Data Modeling; Digital Transformation; Digital Modernization
  - **2020s — Now & Next:** Global Warming; Solution Consulting; Business Consulting; Generalist; Application Rationalization; Business Architecture; AI-Assisted Development; "AI…?" (last item in violet, with the question mark slowly pulsing)

**Footer**
- A pill-shaped badge with a pulsing green dot: "Available for projects and fixed-term deliverables".
**Responsive behaviour**
- Fill the full viewport height, with the content in a column up to 1400px wide.
- Use two columns of cards below 900px wide, and one stacked column with a vertical rail below 560px.
- Keep a side gutter of at least 16px and never scroll horizontally.
- Turn off the animations when the visitor's system is set to reduce motion (prefers-reduced-motion).
